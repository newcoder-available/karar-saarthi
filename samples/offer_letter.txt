OFFER LETTER

Dear Priya Sharma,

We are pleased to offer you the position of Associate Software Engineer at Nimbus Techworks Private Limited, Gurugram, on the following terms.

1. Compensation: Your annual cost to company (CTC) will be Rs. 6,00,000/-. The break-up is provided in Annexure 1.

2. Probation: You will be on probation for six (6) months, during which either party may terminate employment with seven days' notice.

3. Service Agreement: You will undergo a training programme. You are required to serve the Company for a minimum of two (2) years. If you resign before completing two years, you shall pay liquidated damages of Rs. 2,00,000/- as training cost to the Company.

4. Documents: You shall deposit your original educational certificates and mark sheets with HR, which will be retained until completion of the service agreement period.

5. Notice Period: After confirmation, you must give ninety (90) days' notice of resignation. If you leave without serving the full notice period, your salary for the last month and full and final settlement shall be forfeited.

6. Non-compete: You shall not join or work for any competitor or client of the Company for a period of one (1) year after leaving the Company.

7. Intellectual Property: All intellectual property, inventions and work product created by you, whether or not during working hours, shall belong exclusively to the Company.

8. Working Hours: Standard working hours are 9:30 am to 6:30 pm, Monday to Friday. You are entitled to 18 days of paid leave per year.

9. Disputes: The courts at Gurugram shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Please sign and return a copy of this letter as your acceptance.

This Rent Agreement is made at New Delhi on this ____ day of __________ 2026 between Mr. Rakesh Malhotra, Aadhaar No. 2345 6789 0123, mobile 9876543210, residing at B-14, Greater Kailash, New Delhi (hereinafter called the "Landlord") and Ms. Ananya Verma, PAN ABCDE1234F, (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

1. Premises: The Landlord agrees to let out the 2BHK flat at Second Floor, C-221, Lajpat Nagar-II, New Delhi to the Tenant for residential purposes only.

2. Term: The tenancy shall be for a period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st October 2026.

3. Rent: The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty Five Thousand only) per month on or before the 5th day of each English calendar month. Electricity and water charges shall be paid separately by the Tenant as per actual meter reading.

4. Late Payment: In case of delay in payment of rent, the Tenant shall pay interest at the rate of 5% per month on the outstanding amount.

5. Security Deposit: The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 1,50,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only). The security deposit shall be non-refundable if the Tenant vacates the premises before the expiry of the lock-in period. Painting and cleaning charges equal to one month's rent shall be deducted from the security deposit at the time of vacating.

6. Lock-in: There shall be a lock-in period of eleven (11) months. If the Tenant leaves during the lock-in period, the Tenant shall be liable to pay rent for the entire remaining lock-in period and the security deposit shall stand forfeited.

7. Rent Increase: The Landlord may increase the rent at any time during the tenancy at his sole discretion, and the Tenant shall pay the enhanced rent.

8. Repairs: All repairs of any kind, including structural repairs, plumbing and seepage, shall be borne by the Tenant at his own cost.

9. Inspection: The Landlord or his representatives shall have the right to enter the premises at any time without notice for inspection.

10. Default: If the Tenant fails to pay rent for one month, the Landlord shall be entitled to disconnect electricity and water supply and lock the premises, and remove the belongings of the Tenant, and the Tenant shall vacate the premises immediately.

11. Termination: The Landlord may terminate this agreement at any time without assigning any reason. The Tenant shall give three months' written notice to terminate.

12. Changes: The Landlord reserves the right to modify these terms at any time.

13. Disputes: The Tenant shall not approach any court, police or authority in respect of any dispute under this agreement. Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Landlord.

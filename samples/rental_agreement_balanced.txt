LEAVE AND LICENCE / RENT AGREEMENT

This agreement is made at New Delhi between Mr. Rakesh Malhotra (the "Landlord") and Ms. Ananya Verma (the "Tenant").

1. Premises: The Landlord lets the 2BHK flat at Second Floor, C-221, Lajpat Nagar-II, New Delhi to the Tenant for residential use, together with the fittings listed in Annexure A (inventory).

2. Term: The tenancy shall be for a period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st October 2026, renewable by mutual written consent.

3. Rent: The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000/- per month on or before the 7th day of each month by bank transfer.

4. Late Payment: If rent is delayed beyond the 10th of the month, a flat late fee of Rs. 500 shall apply.

5. Security Deposit: The Tenant has paid an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 50,000/- (two months' rent). The deposit shall be refunded within 15 days of the Tenant handing over vacant possession, after deducting only unpaid dues and the cost of damage beyond normal wear and tear, with a written itemised statement and bills.

6. Lock-in: There shall be a lock-in period of three (3) months applicable to both parties. Either party ending the tenancy during the lock-in shall pay one month's rent as compensation.

7. Rent Revision: On renewal, the rent may be increased by 5% with three months' prior written notice. No increase shall be made during the term.

8. Repairs: Structural repairs, whitewashing, seepage and major plumbing shall be done by the Landlord. Minor day-to-day repairs such as bulbs, tap washers and fuses shall be done by the Tenant.

9. Inspection: The Landlord may enter the premises after giving at least 24 hours' written notice, between 8 am and 8 pm, except in an emergency.

10. Termination: Either party may terminate this agreement after the lock-in by giving one (1) month's written notice.

11. Stamp duty and registration charges shall be shared equally.

12. Disputes: The parties shall first try to resolve disputes amicably. Failing that, disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at New Delhi.

                How Karar-Sarthi works

                1. Private by default. Your file is read in memory. Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, emails and bank details are masked before any AI call. Nothing about your document is saved.
                2. Rules first, AI second. A curated Indian-law checklist (the "Fairness Benchmark") finds risky clauses and cites the exact law. Gemini then explains the document in plain language and can spot other unusual terms.
                3. Evidence-locked. Every warning quotes your document word for word. If the AI's quote doesn't match your document, the warning is thrown away. Every answer must cite clause numbers.
                4. Knows its limits. It won't predict court outcomes or tell you what to decide. If your document looks like a legal notice or summons, it points you to free legal aid straight away.

                Built with Gemini on Vertex AI, Cloud Vision (reading photos), Cloud Translation (11 Indian languages), Cloud Text-to-Speech (read aloud), Firestore (anonymous feedback), Cloud Logging and Cloud Run.
